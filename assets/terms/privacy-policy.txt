WEBWIRE LITE PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Updated: January 1, 2025

Byte Motive Data Systems, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the WebWire Lite browser extension ("Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Personal Information
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including but not limited to:
- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Browsing history or website content
- Personal files or documents
- User credentials or API keys

Error Reporting Information
We use Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service, to help us identify and fix technical issues. When the extension encounters an error, Sentry may automatically collect:
- Error messages and stack traces
- Browser and extension version information
- Timestamp of when the error occurred
- General system information (operating system, browser type)

This error data does not include any personal information, webpage content, or user data.

Local Storage
The extension stores configuration settings locally on your device, including:
- AI provider API keys (stored locally only, never transmitted to our servers)
- User preferences and settings
- Conversation history (stored locally only)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The limited information we collect through Sentry is used solely to:
- Identify and diagnose technical problems
- Improve the stability and performance of the extension
- Monitor the overall health of the Service

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purposes beyond maintaining and improving the Service.

INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer any information to third parties, except:
- Sentry error reporting data, which is processed according to Sentry's privacy policy
- When required by law or to protect our legal rights

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Sentry
Our error reporting is powered by Sentry (sentry.io). Error data is processed according to Sentry's Privacy Policy, available at https://sentry.io/privacy/. Sentry may store error data on their servers for the purpose of error analysis and reporting.

AI Service Providers
When you use the extension to communicate with AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.), your interactions are governed by those providers' respective privacy policies. We do not have access to or control over these communications.

DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data we process. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

YOUR RIGHTS

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete from our systems. All your data is stored locally on your device and can be managed through your browser's extension settings.

To clear all local data:
1. Go to your browser's Extensions page
2. Find WebWire Lite and click "Remove"
3. This will delete all locally stored settings and data

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
